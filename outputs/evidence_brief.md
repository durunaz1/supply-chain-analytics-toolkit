# Strategic sourcing intelligence — evidence brief

Source: World Bank Group corporate procurement award CSV.

## Verified descriptive coverage
- Usable award records: 4,299 of 4,799
- Calendar-year range: 2013-07-17 to 2026-06-30
- Published award value in usable records: USD 2,774,081,039
- Distinct named suppliers (not consolidated corporate groups): 2,739
- Distinct commodity categories: 42
- Latest observed calendar year: 2026

## Decision screening
- Largest eligible category by awarded value in latest year: CONTRACT CONSULTANTS
- HHI measures concentration of **published award values**, not market concentration or disruption probability.
- Supplier reallocation scenarios are mathematical counterfactuals, **not feasible procurement plans or savings estimates**.

## Recommended diligence before any sourcing action
1. Confirm supplier legal entities and corporate relationships; aliases can bias concentration.
2. Verify whether award scopes, contract terms and categories are genuinely comparable.
3. Obtain supplier capacity, delivery service levels, qualification, and unit-price quotes before recommending a switch.

## Material limitations
- Published awards exclude smaller contracts and the reporting threshold changes over time; trends are not a complete spend series.
- Some award values are estimates for indefinite-delivery contracts, not actual payments.
- No unit quantities, realized prices, lead times, delivery outcomes, or actual savings are provided.
- No causal inference about supplier selection or diversification is possible.
